## Engine hooks

Each hook is inert unless a move carries its key; the Gate A golden fixture replays unchanged.

| Key | Gate | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `arrivalCond` | B.2 | a forced swap applies this condition to the arriving monster |
| `refusedEffect` | B.2 (hit/both/chance in B.4) | a refused swap move falls back to a condition and/or a hit on the front |
| `hitMult (bench swap)` | B.3 | arrival-hit multiplier for a bench swap move (was the back-row rule 0.5x) |
| `dodgeEntry` | B.3 | entry dodge re-arm rule: 'once' | 'notVoluntary' |
| `breakCharge` | B.4 | breaking a shield charges the attacker's next strike |
| `firstAfterEntry bench exclusion` | B.4 | a Disruptor whose move fires on its first strike after entry never acts from the bench (no default move is affected) |
| `forgeOnHit` | B.5 | a hit that takes at least `min` of the monster's HP adds a forge step (shared cap with the strike forge) |
| `healCut / healCutFor` | B.5 | while the move's condition lasts on a target (at most healCutFor exchanges), heals on it are multiplied by healCut |
| `opening` | B.5 | the move fires once at exchange 1 from any position (its strike trigger becomes once) |
| `cycloneArrival` | B.5 | a sweep applies the move's condition to the monster pulled forward; if the sweep is refused, to the front |
| `sweepHit` | B.5 | the monster a Balanced sweep pulls forward takes this arrival hit |
| `hasteOnSwap` | B.5 | a successful bench swap lets the swapping side's front act first that exchange |
| `echoFlat` | B.5 | the named strike adds this share of the last damage the attacker took, then the echo is spent |
| `stripOnCond, afflict.chanceAbove, swapKind mostHurt` | B.5 | tested hooks used only by rejected variants (inert; kept for reproducibility) |
