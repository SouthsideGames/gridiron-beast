## Football: Blink

Ability ID: `PSY-06-F`. Category: Special action.

Once per game, after 8 net rushing yards on current rushing play, first new tackle attempt arms and triggers teleport if source able. Handoffs and pitches qualify. No reception or kick return.

Blink / Teleport source and carried football through player bodies to clear landing. Solid walls/environmental obstacles block swept path. Successful relocation avoids triggering tackle; normal pursuit and subsequent tackle attempts resume at destination.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per game, after 8 net rushing yards on current rushing play, first new tackle attempt arms and triggers teleport if source able. Handoffs and pitches qualify. No reception or kick return.

### Targets / placement

Destination 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 yards by mastery straight toward offensive endzone at same lateral coordinate. Clear legal landing required. Can reach goal line and score.

### Core resolution

Teleport source and carried football through player bodies to clear landing. Solid walls/environmental obstacles block swept path. Successful relocation avoids triggering tackle; normal pursuit and subsequent tackle attempts resume at destination.

### Recharge / frequency

Once game spent only successful teleport. Illegal path/landing preserves use, original tackle resolves and no further Blink attempt that play. Retain use state through halftime and overtime.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate relocation and authoritative rushing-yard credit once. Eight-yard threshold must be earned on current play. Source immobilized or already-resolving tackle prevents activation.

### Exceptions / counterplay

No sideways alternate landing search. Stunned and Knocked Down prevent. Other movement modifiers apply again after landing. Boundary and normal goal-line scoring rules apply.

### Visuals

Bright star collapses at origin and blooms destination. Dotted psychic trace shows football travel. Source story uses star-spirit future sight.

### Progression / notes

Owner increased original 2/3/4/5-yard distance to 5/6/7/8, allowed crossing player bodies and goal-line scoring. All twelve A approved.

### Ability story

Astralux runs toward a future it has already seen. When a defender reaches the place where it should be, Astralux has stepped into the next moment.

## Beast Clash: Astral Strike

Ability ID: `PSY-06-C`. Category: Signature action.

Once per Clash while active and able, use own normal action to command every eligible living enemy reserve to basic-attack their own active teammate, then perform Astralux own attack.

Command enemy reserves to basic-attack own active at50/60/70/80%, Confused may self-hit. Ordinary defenses/passive survival apply, attack retaliations and chains suppressed. Then own100% attack.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per Clash while active and able, use own normal action to command every eligible living enemy reserve to basic-attack their own active teammate, then perform Astralux own attack.

### Targets / placement

Enemy reserve roster attacks initial enemy active. Stunned reserves cannot attack. Reserves stay reserve. Snapshot stable lineup order for deterministic sequence. No eligible reserve means ordinary Astralux attack and preserve signature.

### Core resolution

Each reserve one basic attack at 50 / 60 / 70 / 80% own normal damage by Astralux mastery. Apply normal accuracy/type/shield/damage defenses and applicable status/damage modifiers separately. After commands, Astralux performs 100% ordinary normal attack.

### Recharge / frequency

Once Clash use spent when first commanded attack launches even evaded. No extra signature cooldown or reserve signature usage. Commanded attacks cannot activate signatures, counters or extra-attack chains.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate ability sequence with no new shared status. If initial enemy active knocked out early, cancel remaining commands, perform ordinary replacement handling, then Astralux attacks replacement. If no living enemy remains, normal victory ends sequence.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Each commanded Confused reserve rolls its ordinary confusion chance. On misfire it attacks itself instead of own active teammate. No recursive confusion roll. Commanded attacks use ordinary shields, Guarded and passive survival effects. Attack retaliation and extra-attack chains remain suppressed. Resolve each whole attack before next commanded attacker. Stunned reserves cannot attack. Each remains in reserve, with no false active-entry effects.

### Visuals

Psychic threads connect Astralux to each enemy reserve. Violet eyes flash, reserves launch coordinated attacks, then Astralux emerges through astral doorway for final strike. No false active-entry events.

### Progression / notes

Striker retained. Commanded damage50/60/70/80%, own attack100%. Owner closeout5–6 resolves Confused self-hit and defenses. All ten original A remain applicable.

### Ability story

Astralux reaches into the minds waiting behind the frontline. For one coordinated strike they see their active teammate as the enemy, and Astralux joins the assault through an astral doorway.
