## Football: Surefoot Canopy

Ability ID: `NAT-04-F`. Category: Targeted effect.

Random teammate protection before each defensive snap

One randomly chosen teammate immune to Stumbled for whole play

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Each defensive SCRIMMAGE play before snap; no cooldown.

### Targets / placement

One OTHER participating defensive teammate, every type eligible, equal chance; exclude Florant. Repeats allowed. Record choice; reload cannot reroll.

### Core resolution

Selected teammate cannot receive ANY Stumbled application that play, any source, regardless distance. No other status immunity/cleanse/attribute modification. Protect every attempt, not only first.

### Recharge / frequency

Every defensive scrimmage play, no charges/cooldown; no forced mastery scaling approved.

### Lifetime / persistence

Protection ends whistle. Ability protection NOT shared status, occupies no status slot.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Both teams see target before snap. No range requirement or moving aura. Old proximity Surefoot proposal replaced. Interaction with already-active Stumbled needs clarification if can persist into this snap.

### Visuals

Leaf effect identifies target, fades whistle. No environmental patch.

### Progression / notes

Uncommon standalone S; Support Clash. Targeted effect category explicitly approved; not Aura.

## Beast Clash: Canopy Assist

Ability ID: `NAT-04-C`. Category: Support.

Once normal turn calls reserve basic attack

Reserve ally attacks without active entry or extra normal turn

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per Clash on Florant normal turn able to act. Eligible non-Stunned living reserve required; none -> Florant attacks normally and keeps use.

### Targets / placement

Reserve with highest CURRENT attack damage, ties lineup order, attacks opposing active. Florant remains active. Target comparison metric must be deterministic.

### Core resolution

Replace Florant attack; assist basic damage 75/80/85/90% normal ally attack. No signature, charge consumption or offensive archetype perks. Normal type/target defenses/evasion. Miss spends use.

### Recharge / frequency

ONCE per Clash, no reset on entry/revival.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate assist then return original reserve slot; not active entry/rotation/normal turn. No status/cooldown/attack-counter ticking for helper.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Eligible retaliation targets ASSISTING ally and resolves normally; no automatic immunity. Entry and rotation effects do not trigger. Need attack-source identity and reaction tag distinct from no-retaliation reaction abilities.

### Visuals

Florant antlers form leafy arch; helper rushes through, attacks, retreats; arch dissolves.

### Progression / notes

Support retained. Mastery damage 75/80/85/90%; no extra resources/attribute boosts.
