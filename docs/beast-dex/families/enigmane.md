## Football: Mind Game

Ability ID: `PSY-05-F`. Category: Targeted effect.

Four participating offensive plays in which Enigmane does not receive the ball fill a four-segment charge bar. Owner approved charge basis and play-long decoy chase. Working draft counts nonconsecutive scrimmage plays without reception, including runs and incomplete targets.

Guaranteed psychic afterimage continues original route direction. Assigned defender chases until anyone catches football including an interception or whistle, whichever first. Then resumes ordinary behavior from its current position. Existing immobilization still constrains movement. Spend full bar on valid activation. No separate chance roll or cooldown. Activation play cannot earn charge, following participating plays can. Maximum one full bar.

### Recharge / frequency

Spend full bar on valid activation. No separate chance roll or cooldown. Activation play cannot earn charge, following participating plays can. Maximum one full bar.

### Lifetime / persistence

Charge partial/full persists possessions, halftime and overtime until actual game end. Afterimage chase ends catch/whistle, not after a second-based timer.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Other defenders still cover real source. Ability effect uses no shared status slot. Physical movement constraints remain. Image carries no football and does not create a second roster monster. Afterimage safe-path representation and other-control priority need implementation audit.

### Visuals

Visible psychic copy continues straight while real Enigmane cuts. Four-segment charge alternates with status badge using shared UI. Real ball and controlled-monster indicators remain accurate.

### Progression / notes

### Ability story

Every time the ball goes somewhere else, Enigmane thinks it was not open enough. After four qualifying plays, its psychic mind sends a copy forward and lets the real tight end break away.
