## Football: Clean Sweep

Ability ID: `EAR-01-F`. Category: Special action.

Ordinary pancake earns later guarantee

Ordinary successful pancake banks one guaranteed pancake on a later participating play

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

An ordinary successful pancake earns one stored guarantee for a LATER participating offensive scrimmage play. Entire family specializes at OL.

### Targets / placement

First legal pancake opportunity on a later participating play; normal reach and engagement required. If no opportunity, preserve guarantee.

### Core resolution

Guaranteed success without pancake failure or stumble roll. Normal pancake Knocked Down recovery rules remain; explicit Knocked Down immunity still applies.

### Recharge / frequency

No additional cooldown. Maximum one stored guarantee. A guaranteed pancake NEVER earns another guarantee.

### Lifetime / persistence

Preserve through possession changes and halftime; clear at actual game end. Same mechanic across all evolutions and mastery.

### Exceptions / counterplay

No teleportation or extra reach. Use normal once-per-play pancake attempt budget. If current status/immunity blocks Knocked Down, skip that target and preserve guarantee until a legal opportunity; no shared-status override.

### Visuals

Stone-fist readiness indicator, forceful shoulder slam with stone fragments on use, ordinary pancake badge. Alternate readiness with shared badge when necessary.

### Progression / notes

Foundation zone and same-play bonus-attempt variants WITHDRAWN. This is not guaranteed pancakes every play.

## Beast Clash: Emergency Cover

Ability ID: `EAR-01-C`. Category: Lethal-hit interception.

Reserve ally would lose active teammate to eligible direct hit

Reserve source intercepts one lethal direct hit damage without rotating or taking attached effects

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per Clash, living reserve Pebblit intervenes when a connected eligible direct hit would KO its active teammate after defenses. Stunned source cannot intervene. Cannot protect self.

### Targets / placement

Original active target resolves evasion first. Predict lethality before applying damage or spending survival abilities. Direct normal/special/follow-up hits eligible; DOT, transferred damage and retaliation excluded.

### Core resolution

Redirect DAMAGE ONLY to reserve source; recalculate against its own type, shield and applicable defenses. NO active-only Tank guard in reserve. No second evasion or consumption of attacker resources. Source can KO.

### Recharge / frequency

Once per Clash, no normal action cost. Remain in reserve; no positions change (owner 6B). Rotation / revival do not restore spent use.

### Lifetime / persistence

Only triggering hit is intercepted. Remaining multi-hit strikes stay aimed at original active monster; original attached effects also remain aimed there under normal rules (owner 8B).

### Exceptions / counterplay

No heal, invulnerability or guaranteed survival. Multiple eligible reserve rescuers and same-hit survival ordering require deterministic audit resolution.

### Visuals

Reserve source visibly intercepts impact, then returns to its reserve position; do not animate a permanent active-position swap.

### Progression / notes

Tank retained. No progression scaling approved. Distinct from setup-based Shared Burden damage splitting and self-only Last Root.
