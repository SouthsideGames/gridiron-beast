## Football: Mirror Step

Ability ID: `PSY-02-F`. Category: Self enhancement.

While ready and able on man coverage, assigned receiver first designated route cut before pass release can activate if in range.

Mirror Step / Psychically mirror receiver new route direction without ordinary turning slowdown, retaining current movement speed. Existing separation still must be covered with ordinary movement.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

While ready and able on man coverage, assigned receiver first designated route cut before pass release can activate if in range.

### Targets / placement

Assigned man receiver only. Wispira within 3 yards, Mirageist within 4 yards at cut. No zone coverage fallback.

### Core resolution

Psychically mirror receiver new route direction without ordinary turning slowdown, retaining current movement speed. Existing separation still must be covered with ordinary movement.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 subsequent participating defensive plays by mastery after activation. No qualifying cut, out of range or unable source preserves readiness.

### Lifetime / persistence

One cut resolution only. No timed buff, status or ongoing attribute modification. Ordinary game persistence applies to running cooldown.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Normal bodies, walls, environmental movement penalties and existing immobilization apply. Only ordinary turning slowdown is bypassed. No teleport, collision bypass or guaranteed breakup.

### Visuals

Psychic ripple links receiver and cornerback briefly. Curved lavender afterimage marks the mirrored turn, with no extra actor or status badge.

### Progression / notes

Form improves range from 3 to 4 yards. Mastery changes cooldown. Approved all ten A. Family remains CB and Common through evolution.

### Ability story

Wispira listens to the receiver intent through a psychic connection, matching one sharp cut before ordinary footwork can slow it.

## Beast Clash: Mirage Veil

Ability ID: `PSY-02-C`. Category: Signature action.

Two completed enemy direct normal attacks from same attacker damage source HP while active and ready. Resolve whole second attack and attached effects first. Must survive and remain able.

Mirage Veil / Attempt Guarded 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% reduction by mastery for 2 owner normal turns, application turn excluded. Normal shared status gate and same-status application rules apply.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Two completed enemy direct normal attacks from same attacker damage source HP while active and ready. Resolve whole second attack and attached effects first. Must survive and remain able.

### Targets / placement

Self Guarded attempt. Count whole attack once including multihit. A different attacker HP hit begins count 1 for new attacker. Miss and shield-only attack neither count nor reset.

### Core resolution

Attempt Guarded 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% reduction by mastery for 2 owner normal turns, application turn excluded. Normal shared status gate and same-status application rules apply.

### Recharge / frequency

Five subsequent owner normal turns cooldown, paused in reserve. No charging during cooldown. A qualifying attempt spends readiness even if status blocked. Unable source preserves readiness, discards trigger and queues no application.

### Lifetime / persistence

Enemy attacker departure resets same-attacker progress. Guarded duration and cooldown pause in reserve under shared rules. Guarded clears on football entry per existing unsupported-mode policy.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Protection begins only after triggering attack resolves, never prevents that attack lethal damage. No new shield pool, decoy evasion or retaliation.

### Visuals

Two faint psychic outlines gather after qualifying attacks and merge into protective veil. Standard Guarded badge displays.

### Progression / notes

Both forms share rules and mastery values. Approved Support to Tank change for whole family. Permanent ratings supply evolution durability difference.

### Ability story

Wispira learns the rhythm of repeated strikes and wraps itself in a veil tuned to survive the next assault.
