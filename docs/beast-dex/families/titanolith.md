## Football: Landslide

Ability ID: `EAR-06-F`. Category: Special action.

First eligible offensive snap each possession

Snap slam creates advancing full-front rock wall with guaranteed bounded displacement

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per offensive possession at first eligible offensive scrimmage snap, run or pass. Stunned / Knocked Down prevents activation, preserving use for later snap that possession.

### Targets / placement

At snap, target defenders aligned within TWO yards on defensive side of LOS, including front-aligned edge rushers/blitzers. Wall spans the full qualified front, outermost to outermost; rear off-ball defenders are not included.

### Core resolution

Guaranteed push 2 / 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 yards toward defenders own end zone by mastery. NO Strength contest. Explicit displacement immunity and physical obstructions still apply.

### Recharge / frequency

Firing spends possession use even if immunity prevents movement. Special teams and two-point attempts excluded.

### Lifetime / persistence

Wall advances through approved push distance and immediately crumbles. No lingering blocker or extra status; normal defender actions resume after push.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Instant action / physical wall, not Earth zone; no same-type field immunity. Cannot push through obstruction or award automatic rushing yards. Remove legacy guaranteed pass-block wins and +2 rush yards.

### Visuals

Both fists strike turf; continuous stone wall erupts across front, surges forward carrying targets back, fractures into fading rubble.

### Progression / notes

Legendary standalone OL. Owner approved two-yard snap-alignment frontline definition. Keep guaranteed bounded push at 2 / 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 yards and explicit obstruction/immunity handling.

## Beast Clash: Mountain Shelter

Ability ID: `EAR-06-C`. Category: Team Guarded replacement.

Own action when any living teammate at <=50 percent HP

Once normal action guarantees team Guarded overriding all prior shared statuses

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per Clash replace normal action when any living teammate INCLUDING self is <=50% HP. Otherwise ordinary attack, preserve use. Stunned prevents activation and preserves readiness.

### Targets / placement

EVERY living teammate, including source and reserves, GUARANTEED. No eligible-other-recipient prerequisite. Never revive KO monsters.

### Core resolution

Guarded reduces incoming attacks/reactions/DOT by 30 / 35 / 40 / 45% by mastery. Explicit owner exception: REPLACE existing harmful or beneficial status, even uncleansable; remove replaced Shielded pool. One active status/badge remains.

### Recharge / frequency

Once per Clash; no refresh on rotation or revival. Uses normal action instead of attack. No damage reflection, HP healing or shield pool.

### Lifetime / persistence

Two RECIPIENT normal turns, application turn excluded. Reserve pauses; Stunned-skipped turns count. Protection independent after source leaves or KO. Clear on entering football under existing unsupported-mode rule.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Guaranteed replacement also applies to already Guarded allies: replace with a FRESH episode at Titanolith mastery strength and TWO recipient normal turns, even if old Guarded was stronger/longer. No additive old duration; bypass old episode application cap. Other later applications follow normal shared status rules.

### Visuals

Stone arches briefly rise over each protected monster then dissolve into standard Guarded badge. No persistent canopy clutter.

### Progression / notes

Tank retained. No blanket change to other cleanse/stack rules. Original largest entry shield / immovable legacy signature replaced.
