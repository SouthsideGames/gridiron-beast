## Football: Mind Bend

Ability ID: `PSY-01-F`. Category: Zone.

Personal tackle or assist after an opponent completed pass while ready banks a Psychic zone for the next eligible defensive pre-choice opportunity.

Mind Bend / Non-Psychic monsters of both teams inside receive inward movement vector at 20% of applicable maximum speed. Stop pull within 1 yard of center. Ordinary movement can overcome pull. Psychic monsters have same-type neutrality.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Personal tackle or assist after an opponent completed pass while ready banks a Psychic zone for the next eligible defensive pre-choice opportunity.

### Targets / placement

8 yards wide sideline-to-sideline and 6 yards long endzone-to-endzone. At upcoming snap place from LOS+3 to LOS+9 toward opponent goal, centered laterally on ball. Near endzone shift toward LOS only enough to fit in field of play.

### Core resolution

Non-Psychic monsters of both teams inside receive inward movement vector at 20% of applicable maximum speed. Stop pull within 1 yard of center. Ordinary movement can overcome pull. Psychic monsters have same-type neutrality.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 6 / 5 / 4 / 3 subsequent participating defensive scrimmage plays by mastery, starts after creation. Cannot bank during cooldown. Unable source preserves pending readiness.

### Lifetime / persistence

Created zone life 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 defensive scrimmage plays by mastery including creation play and incompletions. Pending survives possessions and halftime. Created zone clears at halftime and remains stationary after creation.

### Exceptions / counterplay

No status application, automatic stun, player-targeted dragging or football displacement. Physical collision and field bounds apply. Placement revealed before both choices and cannot move on audible.

### Visuals

Transparent magenta rectangle, concentric rings and inward-flowing particles. Ball, routes and players stay readable.

### Progression / notes

Same dimensions and effect by form. Mastery controls life and cooldown only. Approved in chat all ten A.

### Ability story

Psyflutter senses the flow of a completed pass and folds the next defensive space inward, letting its Psychic family move freely through the bend.

## Beast Clash: Mind Mend

Ability ID: `PSY-01-C`. Category: Signature action.

On own normal action while active, able and ready, replace attack to donate own HP to an injured living teammate.

Mind Mend / Donate 10% of source maximum HP for Psyflutter or 15% for Psyraptor, plus mastery 0 / 2 / 4 / 6 percentage points. Restore one target HP per donated HP before applicable healing penalties. Limit donation to target missing HP and source current HP minus 1.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

On own normal action while active, able and ready, replace attack to donate own HP to an injured living teammate.

### Targets / placement

Lowest current HP-percentage injured living teammate including reserves, excluding self. Stable lineup ordering resolves equal percentages.

### Core resolution

Donate 10% of source maximum HP for Psyflutter or 15% for Psyraptor, plus mastery 0 / 2 / 4 / 6 percentage points. Restore one target HP per donated HP before applicable healing penalties. Limit donation to target missing HP and source current HP minus 1.

### Recharge / frequency

Four subsequent owner normal turns cooldown, paused in reserve. No eligible target or affordable positive donation preserves readiness and uses ordinary attack if able. Stunned source follows normal skip.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate donation and healing. Existing statuses remain. No ongoing schedule. State follows ordinary once-Clash boundaries, cooldown survives rotation.

### Exceptions / counterplay

HP cost cannot reduce source below 1 HP. Healing Reduced affects recipient normally. Donation does not cleanse, revive or generate surplus shield. Final rounding uses shared-system approved convention when reconciled.

### Visuals

Glowing orb leaves source chest. Source HP falls, then teammate HP rises. No new status badge.

### Progression / notes

Effective donation percentages by mastery: Psyflutter 10 / 12 / 14 / 16; Psyraptor 15 / 17 / 19 / 21. Archetype Support. Approved all ten A.

### Ability story

Psyflutter offers some of its own vitality so an injured teammate can return to the fight, accepting vulnerability to protect the team.
