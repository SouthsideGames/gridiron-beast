## Football: Phase Release

Ability ID: `ELE-03-F`. Category: Self enhancement.

Ready receiving-route snap

Temporary collision-and-jam bypass until first cut or pass release

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Automatically activate at a ready receiving-route snap; activation spends readiness even with no contact.

### Targets / placement

Zappard only. No teammate protection or persistent field.

### Core resolution

Ignore opposing body collisions and jams while phased; cannot block opponents during that state. No teleportation or Speed increase.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 subsequent offensive plays.

### Lifetime / persistence

Ends at the first designated route cut, QB pass release or whistle, whichever occurs first. Solidify before ball flight and resolve catches, interceptions and tackles normally.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Defenders track normally; no automatic separation or confusion. Fields and statuses still apply. Stunned or Knocked Down prevents activation or ends phasing.

### Visuals

Readable electrical silhouette while phased, returning to solid form at the endpoint.

### Progression / notes

Ability state, not an additional shared status. Mastery changes cooldown only.

## Beast Clash: Phase Strike

Ability ID: `ELE-03-C`. Category: Shield bypass.

Active enemy Shielded on eligible own action

Once-only attack bypasses shield absorption without removing shield pool

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once per Clash, replace a normal attack when the active enemy is Shielded. No eligible target: ordinary attack and preserve use.

### Targets / placement

Active enemy only; its existing shield remains in place.

### Core resolution

Deal 100% / 110% / 120% / 130% normal attack damage directly to HP, bypassing shield absorption ONLY. Do not damage or strip the shield pool.

### Recharge / frequency

Once per Clash; normal evasion / decoys apply and a miss spends the use.

### Lifetime / persistence

Single special attack; no persistent mode or buff.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Type matchups, damage reduction, Tank guard, Guarded, hit caps and Last Root still work. Damage-triggered retaliation applies; shield-break triggers do not. No ordinary-only Striker shield-break perk on this special.

### Visuals

Electrical body passes through the visible shield, strikes the target and returns; shield remains visibly intact.

### Progression / notes

Striker retained. Unlike Wash Away, this does not remove a beneficial status.
