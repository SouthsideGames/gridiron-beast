## Football: Permafrost

Ability ID: `ICE-04-F`. Category: Zone.

Personal TFL or sack earns later backfield Ice zone

Personal negative-yardage stop banks fixed quarterback-aligned backfield braking patch

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Personal tackle-for-loss or sack while ready; assisted credit qualifies, zero-yard stop does not. Primary position DL, no evolution.

### Targets / placement

Next participating defensive scrimmage pre-play: TEN yards wide by SIX yards long, from upcoming LOS SIX yards into OFFENSIVE BACKFIELD. Lateral center QB initial alignment. Reveal before both play choices, then lock; ordinary boundary rules.

### Core resolution

30% reduced braking deceleration for both teams non-Ice. Ice unaffected. Normal maximum Speed/acceleration. No auto Stumbled/Stunned or automatic yardage subtraction.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5/4/3/2 subsequent defensive plays starts CREATION. Cannot earn again during creation play; max ONE pending zone.

### Lifetime / persistence

TWO completed defensive scrimmage plays INCLUDING creation. Stationary world location after spawn, not moved with later LOS.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Pending survives possession changes / halftime. Created field clears halftime; actual end clears all. Shared field overlap rules. Live QB can move out; defenders chasing into field also suffer braking penalty.

### Visuals

Both front hooves stamp, frost wave spreads across readable flat rectangle. No physical wall.

### Progression / notes

Zone exception permits same Ice effect as Frostmark; distinct stop trigger and fixed backfield placement. Remove old subtract-one-yard Permafrost.

## Beast Clash: Snowdrift Shelter

Ability ID: `ICE-04-C`. Category: Self Guarded preparation.

Ready source at <=50 percent HP and empty shared status slot

Sacrifice attack for one-next-owner-turn self Guarded shelter

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

At <=50% HP while ready and own status slot EMPTY, replace normal attack with preparation. Otherwise ordinary attack and preserve readiness. Stunned prevents setup.

### Targets / placement

Self only. Apply Guarded 50% incoming damage reduction; normal active Tank protection still follows existing scope.

### Core resolution

No heal, cleanse, shield pool, immunity, guaranteed survival or attack bonus. Defense only, no offensive armor cash-out.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5/4/3/2 subsequent owner normal turns begins setup. Mastery cooldown only; protection fixed.

### Lifetime / persistence

Guarded lasts through NEXT owner normal turn and expires at that turn END. Setup turn excluded. On next turn emerge and perform ordinary legal action. Reserve pauses status and cooldown.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Normal shared-status rules; later replacement/removal follows those rules. No rotation refresh or source maxHP change.

### Visuals

Snow mound surrounds source with horns visible. Burst out on next action, standard Guarded badge, no second custom status.

### Progression / notes

Tank retained by owner choice. Packed Avalanche and Glacial Claim proposals withdrawn; no protection theft or guard-to-damage conversion.
