## Football: Stone Charge

Ability ID: `EAR-03-F`. Category: Special action.

Ready designed rushing carry with defender three yards ahead

Committed three-yard straight rush attempts one sideways contact displacement

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Designed rushing carry after secured handoff or direct snap. No receptions or returns. Activate while ready when defender within three yards ahead of current rushing direction.

### Targets / placement

First defender contacted during up-to-three-yard committed travel. No contact: still spends readiness.

### Core resolution

Hold current heading until three yards traveled, first contact or whistle. Normal Speed/acceleration. On contact, normal Strength-vs-Strength pushback contest; success moves defender one yard sideways toward nearest legal open side.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 subsequent offensive plays, spent on activation.

### Lifetime / persistence

Commitment ends on contact or whistle / travel limit. No persistent field or shared status.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Failure: ordinary tackle resolution. Success: normal tackle still applies if defender in reach. No automatic tackle break, fumble protection, immunity or credited bonus yards. One contact attempt.

### Visuals

Owner 10B: translucent BOULDER SHELL surrounds source during charge. Do not retain only the original stone-plate/dust version.

### Progression / notes

Uncommon standalone RB. Source legacy +10 carry Strength removed; new ability uses ordinary attributes without adding any.

## Beast Clash: Unbroken Charge

Ability ID: `EAR-03-C`. Category: Stun-response special.

Actually skip normal action due to Stunned

Stunned skipped action arms next performed normal-action undodgeable charge

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Actually losing a normal action to Stunned arms once-per-Clash charge. Merely receiving Stunned is not sufficient.

### Targets / placement

Next performed normal attack targets enemy then active, not necessarily original stun source. Further Stunned skips preserve armed charge.

### Core resolution

150 / 160 / 170 / 180% normal attack by mastery, undodgeable including decoys. Normal type, shield, Guarded, active Tank protection and survival abilities apply.

### Recharge / frequency

Once per Clash, committed when charge FIRST ARMS. Costs next performed normal action; counts as performed attack for normal attack counters. No ordinary-only archetype perks; normal retaliation applies.

### Lifetime / persistence

Armed charge survives rotation; KO clears it. Revival cannot restore a spent use.

### Exceptions / counterplay

No stun immunity, cleanse, bonus action or permanent attribute boost. Arming commits use. KO destroys unused armed charge and revival cannot rearm or restore it.

### Visuals

Glowing cracks when skipped turn arms ability; translucent boulder shell forms as source charges, hits and returns.

### Progression / notes

Tank retained. Do not silently convert charge into immediate reaction when Stunned expires.
