## Football: Dragon Stomp

Ability ID: `DRA-04-F`. Category: Special action.

Three participating offensive plays finishing engaged in block fill charge bar. Next offensive play engaging eligible DL triggers stomp while source able.

Dragon Stomp / Spectral dragon foot guarantees Pancaked attempt, subject to shared status eligibility. Pancake outcome applies existing Knocked Down status for remainder of current play under approved global timing revision.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Three participating offensive plays finishing engaged in block fill charge bar. Next offensive play engaging eligible DL triggers stomp while source able.

### Targets / placement

DL personally blocked by Scalor. Uses existing one-pancake-attempt-per-play budget.

### Core resolution

Spectral dragon foot guarantees Pancaked attempt, subject to shared status eligibility. Pancake outcome applies existing Knocked Down status for remainder of current play under approved global timing revision.

### Recharge / frequency

Full bar spent on stomp attempt even if status blocked. Activation play cannot recharge. Unable source or no eligible DL preserves. If pancake budget already spent, no additional attempt. Partial/full bar survives possessions/half/OT until actual game end.

### Lifetime / persistence

Knockdown ends whistle. No new status identity or seconds-based pancake duration. Existing contact, movement and blocking rules resume when restored next play.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Only charge from completed participating offensive plays finishing engaged. No charge from mere lineup presence. No second normal pancake attempt following stomp.

### Visuals

Giant dragon-foot shadow forms beneath target, then spectral foot slams and flattens defender with existing Knocked Down indicator.

### Progression / notes

Threshold3, once-play pancake budget. No invented mastery scaling. Owner chose 1B and remaining A. Replaces recovery and challenge drafts.

### Ability story

Scalor carries the weight of ancient dragons in its scales. After holding the line long enough, it calls down an ancestor mighty step.

## Beast Clash: Dragonhide

Ability ID: `DRA-04-C`. Category: Signature action.

First active entry once per Clash attempts own Guarded 20/25/30/35% for2 owner normal turns, application turn excluded. Existing different status blocks and spends entry opportunity.

Dragonhide / Convert full Guarded percentage into damage bonus: attack120/125/130/135% normal. Ordinary evasion/type/shields/reduction apply, ordinary-only archetype attack bonuses excluded.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

First active entry once per Clash attempts own Guarded 20/25/30/35% for2 owner normal turns, application turn excluded. Existing different status blocks and spends entry opportunity.

### Targets / placement

Self original Dragonhide Guarded episode only. On own normal action in final remaining protected turn, consume it for enhanced attack against current active enemy.

### Core resolution

Convert full Guarded percentage into damage bonus: attack120/125/130/135% normal. Ordinary evasion/type/shields/reduction apply, ordinary-only archetype attack bonuses excluded.

### Recharge / frequency

One entry application attempt and at most one conversion per Clash. Consumed protection stays spent even if attack evaded. Rotation/revival do not grant fresh application.

### Lifetime / persistence

Reserve pauses Guarded. Removal/replacement loses conversion opportunity. Unable conversion turn follows normal skip/status decrement. If Guarded expires, no queued bonus.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Only original owned Guarded eligible, not external replacement. Conversion replaces normal attack. No extra action, shield pool or healing.

### Visuals

Hardened scales form at entry with Guarded badge. At conversion scales peel away and gather on claws, badge disappears as attack launches.

### Progression / notes

Owner changed original half conversion to full Guarded percentage and timing to final protected turn. Balanced retained. Original redundant four-turn conversion cooldown superseded by once-original-episode limit.

### Ability story

Scalor hardens its scales on entering battle. As protection nears its end, it gathers those scales around its claws and drives them into a final heavy strike.
