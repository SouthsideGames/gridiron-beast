## Football: Bramble Trap

Ability ID: `NAT-05-F`. Category: Zone.

Break receiving tackle earns next-play target-centered patch

Random non-Nature defender-centered next-play Nature zone

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

First successfully broken tackle AFTER RECEPTION while ready. No rushing carries/returns/two-point attempts.

### Targets / placement

Before play selection choose uniformly from participating NON-NATURE defenders in initial pre-play formation. Center stationary8wide x6long patch on chosen defender INITIAL position. Shift inward at boundaries to preserve size. No eligible defender -> hold pending readiness. Later formation/play changes do not reroll target or move zone. Fixed5/12-yard reference bands do NOT replace this explicitly defender-centered placement.

### Core resolution

Nature turning-rate reduction 25/30/35/40% mastery. Straight speed/acceleration unchanged. Both teams non-Nature affected; no immobilization, physical vines, damage or shared status.

### Recharge / frequency

THREE subsequent offensive plays cooldown starts spawn; no re-earn on spawn play. One pending activation, no accumulation.

### Lifetime / persistence

ONE completed play including spawn play, all outcomes. Pending activation survives possession changes/HALFTIME. Spawned nonlegendary zone clears whistle/halftime.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Recorded target and initial position are locked before both teams choose plays; no reload/audible reroll. Three subsequent offensive-play cooldown starts spawn; no re-earn on spawn play or while unavailable. One pending activation persists possession/halftime. Bramble Lane rejected.

### Visuals

Both teams see target/patch before snap; seed drops beneath chosen defender and blooms.

### Progression / notes

Rare standalone TE; Zone. Size8x6 fixed; mastery turning25/30/35/40%.

## Beast Clash: Shell Harvest

Ability ID: `NAT-05-C`. Category: Tank.

First active entry shield; voluntary departure cashes remainder

Voluntary exit converts remaining own shield HP to self healing

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

ONCE first ACTIVE entry attempts Shielded; if status gate blocks attempt spent. No refresh on re-entry/revival.

### Targets / placement

Self; shield pool 20/22/24/26% max HP by mastery, shared Shielded status.

### Core resolution

Successful voluntary departure consumes entire remaining ORIGINAL Shell Harvest shield and heals100% of eligible remaining Shield HP capped missing HP. Excess lost. Other-source replacement Shielded cannot be converted. Resolve pre-departure damage before computing remaining pool. Refused/failed swap does not convert; no break retaliation.

### Recharge / frequency

One entry grant, no reset; conversion requires live shield when voluntary exit succeeds.

### Lifetime / persistence

Shield THREE subsequent own normal turns, application excluded, reserve pauses, Stunned skips count. Forced exit preserves shield/duration, no heal. Depleted/stripped/expired no heal.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Cash out ONLY original Shell Harvest-created shield. If another source REPLACES it, replacement is ineligible. Normal equal Shielded duration extensions do not refill/change pool origin. Resolve any pre-departure Pursuit first; only surviving successful voluntary exit converts remaining eligible pool. No healing if KO before leaving. Normal Healing Reduced applies if legally present; shared slot ordinarily prevents coexistence.

### Visuals

Woven thorn shell; on voluntary exit remaining branches bloom into leaves and dissolve into body.

### Progression / notes

Tank retained; old thorns retaliation replaced. Shield 20/22/24/26%, no added attributes.
