## Football: Deep Roots

Ability ID: `NAT-03-F`. Category: Self enhancement.

First block engagement when ready

Fixed rooted blocking position plus one ordinary-shed prevention

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

First block engagement while ready, run OR pass blocking; not one-yard-push trigger.

### Targets / placement

Self at engagement point; ordinary reach for defenders. Remain stationary until whistle.

### Core resolution

Root in place, no translation; may turn and engage within reach. Prevent all forced displacement including ability pushes. Prevent FIRST successful ORDINARY block shed during activation play; after consumed sheds normal. No attribute changes.

### Recharge / frequency

5/4/3/2 subsequent offensive-play cooldown, starts on root activation.

### Lifetime / persistence

Roots remain through play even if defender gets past. Ends whistle. No field/shared status.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Special bypass such as Vine Vault can succeed and does NOT consume ordinary-shed prevention. Does not grant Stunned/Knocked Down immunity. Branching Guard alternative discarded.

### Visuals

Thick roots from feet into turf; lean against defender, roots taut; retract whistle.

### Progression / notes

Uncommon standalone OL; Self enhancement. Both blocking assignments eligible; old half-speed pass-push behavior replaced.

## Beast Clash: Last Root

Ability ID: `NAT-03-C`. Category: Tank.

Automatic otherwise lethal damage

One lethal damage event prevented leaving one HP

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Once-use available and any incoming damage event would KO. Automatic even Stunned, active OR reserve.

### Targets / placement

Self only. One damage event, not whole multi-hit attack.

### Core resolution

Set surviving HP to ONE instead of KO; prevent event lethal remainder. Includes direct, reaction and Afflicted damage. Can activate from already 1 HP. No heal/shield/cleanse/retaliation. Next hit may KO.

### Recharge / frequency

ONCE per Clash across entries; revival does not refresh spent use.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate survival only. Other statuses/independent effects resolve normally. No temporary invulnerability.

### Exceptions / counterplay

If not spent before KO by an uncovered mechanism, remains unused on revival. Preserve flags; all damage paths need pre-KO hook. Replace old entry shield, normal Tank stats/active guard retained.

### Visuals

Impact bends body, roots brace, bark cracks, one green leaf glows as HP stops at 1; roots retract. No lingering badge.

### Progression / notes

Tank retained; identical survival effect all mastery levels. No added scaling.
