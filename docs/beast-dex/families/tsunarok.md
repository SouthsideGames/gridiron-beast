## Beast Clash: Riptide Pursuit

Ability ID: `WAT-06-C`. Category: Disruptor.

One ready pursuit each active entry

Undodgeable reaction punishes voluntary enemy departure

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Opening active entry and EVERY later active entry grants ONE ready pursuit; never accumulate unused uses. While active and able to act, react to first opposing voluntary rotation before target departs.

### Targets / placement

Departing opposing active monster. Forced rotations and knockout replacements do NOT trigger.

### Core resolution

Reaction deals 75/85/95/105% normal attack damage by mastery and CANNOT be evaded. Normal type, damage reduction and Shield HP apply. Costs no normal action. Surviving target completes rotation; no additional status.

### Recharge / frequency

One use per active entry, not once per Clash. Re-entry replaces unused opportunity rather than stacking. No seconds cooldown.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate pre-departure hit. If target KO, resolve knockout and replacement normally. No delayed mark/status.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Cannot trigger while Stunned; unused opportunity retained. Pursuit cannot trigger counterattacks/retaliation or further reaction chains. Must integrate voluntary/forced/KO entry reasons and existing rotation locks; no football pulling.

### Visuals

Tsunarok briefly transforms into water and surges into departing monster. Damage resolves before lineup transition.

### Progression / notes

Disruptor retained. Opening entry included; new entry max readiness 1. Undodgeable chosen explicitly over initial normal-evasion recommendation.
