## Football: Stonewall Swat

Ability ID: `EAR-02-F`. Category: Targeted effect.

Three low-yardage participating defensive plays

Banked low-yardage charge guarantees next legally launched forward pass becomes incomplete

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Three qualifying participating defensive scrimmage plays fill bar, maximum one charge per play. No personal tackle/assist/PBU required.

### Targets / placement

Qualifying opponent gain <=3 / <=4 / <=5 / <=6 yards by mastery, including losses and incompletions. Opponent touchdowns, special teams and two-point attempts excluded.

### Core resolution

Armed source targets first opposing forward pass RELEASED on a later participating defensive play, including play-action and RPO passing branches. Guaranteed breakup regardless distance; no interception, fumble, player damage or status.

### Recharge / frequency

Launch empties bar. No extra cooldown. Activation play cannot earn charge. Runs preserve readiness.

### Lifetime / persistence

Partial and full bars carry through possessions and halftime; actual game end clears. Craggo and Ramparte share progression thresholds.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Stunned / Knocked Down at release prevents launch and preserves bar. Ordinary block engagement does not prevent it. If multiple able users are armed, stable lineup order selects ONE; only its bar empties, others remain full.

### Visuals

Full charge becomes ready stone icon. At QB release, rock launches, visibly strikes football and causes incomplete pass; rock crumbles, no field obstacle.

### Progression / notes

Rock Roller proposal withdrawn. Incomplete-pass result must flow through ordinary down/clock/stat handling; do not create a catch then reverse it.

## Beast Clash: Rubble Reprisal

Ability ID: `EAR-02-C`. Category: Healing-triggered reaction.

Active enemy receives actual HP healing while source ready

Active source reacts after enemy healing with one rock attack

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

While ready, source active and able to act; Stunned prevents reaction and preserves readiness. Active enemy must receive actual HP restoration, from self, ally or healing effect.

### Targets / placement

Same active enemy that was healed. Healing resolves first. A single healing event can trigger at most one rock.

### Core resolution

Craggo damage 60 / 65 / 70 / 75%; Ramparte 70 / 75 / 80 / 85% normal attack by mastery. Normal evasion, type, shields and applicable damage reduction.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 subsequent owner normal turns; application turn excluded, reserve pauses. Launch spends even on miss.

### Lifetime / persistence

Immediate reaction, not a stored normal attack or extra owner turn.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Shield grants, zero-HP overheal, fully blocked healing, revival and HP redistribution do not trigger. No attack-counter progress, stored-charge consumption, ordinary-only perks or retaliation chain.

### Visuals

Healing finishes; source stamps, raises loose stone and headbutts it toward opponent.

### Progression / notes

Balanced retained. Finish the entire originating healing ability, including shield or rotation effects, BEFORE reaction. Launch only if the same healed opponent remains active and source can act; otherwise preserve readiness.
