## Football: Night Watch

Ability ID: `DAR-02-F`. Category: Aura.

DAR-02-F: Night Watch; Ignore only Awareness reductions from Dark ENVIRONMENTAL field. No bonus outside field, no status cleanse or protection from Confused.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Noctlet; common; S; While active safety is able, allies within 4-yard moving radius benefit on defensive scrimmage plays.

### Targets / placement

Moving radius 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 yards by mastery. Real allied non-Dark defenders inside ignore only the Dark base-field Awareness penalty. No redundant self bonus; Noctlet and Strixen already have same-type field neutrality.

### Core resolution

Ignore only Awareness reductions from Dark ENVIRONMENTAL field. No bonus outside field, no status cleanse or protection from Confused.

### Recharge / frequency

Passive aura; no cooldown or charges.

### Lifetime / persistence

Only while in radius and source able. Leaves no lasting status and no cross-mode payload.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Stunned / Knocked Down source disables. Does not nullify Eclipse denial or other hybrid effects. Dark allies already immune gain nothing.

### Visuals

Low owl-eye ring, allies inspect shows Dark-awareness protection; no head badge.

### Progression / notes

Aura radius 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 yards by mastery. Protection has no additional stat bonus. Need modifier provenance to filter base Dark Awareness term only.

## Beast Clash: Night Swoop

Ability ID: `DAR-02-C`. Category: Clash ability.

DAR-02-C: Night Swoop; Resolve this first normal attack before opponents scheduled in same round at 110 / 120% normal damage by Noctlet / Strixen. It is still evadable and can be Stunned before action scheduling.

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Noctlet; common; S; Clash archetype Balanced → Striker. First performed normal attack of each Clash.

### Targets / placement

Current enemy active target; no reserve hit.

### Core resolution

Resolve this first normal attack before opponents scheduled in same round at 110 / 120% normal damage by Noctlet / Strixen. It is still evadable and can be Stunned before action scheduling.

### Recharge / frequency

Spent on performed attack even if miss; support action or Stunned skip preserves first-attack opportunity.

### Lifetime / persistence

First eligible attack priority applies once; no reset on rotation/revival.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Does not interrupt already-resolving action or beat an explicit higher-priority interrupt. Ordinary damage mitigation and Confused apply.

### Visuals

Owl silhouette arcs overhead then strikes before turn marker settles.

### Progression / notes

Power +0 / +5 / +10 / +15 percentage points by mastery; one use. Need stable priority tier: interrupts first, then priority attacks in ordinary initiative/tie order.
