## Football: Crosswind Rescue

Ability ID: `WIN-04-F`. Category: Zone.

Galyx; uncommon Wind Safety.2 personal pass breakups charge bar; one credit per breakup event. Next eligible defensive scrimmage pre-play creates lanes while source able, before BOTH play selections.

PBU charge creates paired stationary sideline Wind lanes at next eligible defensive snap

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Galyx; uncommon Wind Safety.2 personal pass breakups charge bar; one credit per breakup event. Next eligible defensive scrimmage pre-play creates lanes while source able, before BOTH play selections.

### Targets / placement

TWO stationary sideline rectangles: EACH4 yards WIDE sideline direction ×7 yards LONG endzone direction. Left/right outer edges meet playable sidelines; longitudinal band upcoming LOS+5 throughLOS+12. Normal field-boundary clipping; locked after reveal.

### Core resolution

Non-Wind monsters on BOTH teams gain20% maximum movement speed inside either lane. Acceleration unchanged. Wind monsters neither benefit nor suffer. Environmental modifier, not status.

### Recharge / frequency

Bar empties when pair creates; no extra cooldown; creation play cannot recharge. If own previous lanes remain, hold full bar until they expire. At most1 own created pair,1 full bar, no overflow.

### Lifetime / persistence

Partial/full charge survives possession/half/OT; created nonlegendary lanes clear halftime. Incompletions still count field lifetime. Stationary after creation; no pre-choice reroll/audible reposition.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Source Stunned / Knocked Down prevents creation and preserves bar. Offensive receivers can exploit lanes too. No automatic pursuit win or speed to source Wind family.

### Visuals

Two pale teal bordered gust lanes along sidelines; readable ball, yard marks and routes. Charge/status display alternates.

## Beast Clash: Second Wind

Ability ID: `WIN-04-C`. Category: Reserve priority support.

Galyx; Support. Once per Clash FROM RESERVE before normal round initiative resolves, if living active ally would act second and has an action it can legally perform.

Once reserve support moves active ally existing second action before enemy

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Galyx; Support. Once per Clash FROM RESERVE before normal round initiative resolves, if living active ally would act second and has an action it can legally perform.

### Targets / placement

Current living ACTIVE ally only. Source must remain living/able in reserve at trigger; do not look for another active teammate.

### Core resolution

Advance ally EXISTING normal action before opponent in this round. NO extra action, heal, damage boost or Stunned bypass; source itself does not take a normal attack.

### Recharge / frequency

Spend only when order actually changes. If ally already acts first, is Stunned, or source unable, preserve. No rotation/revival refresh.

### Lifetime / persistence

Priority change applies current round only. If target becomes KO/unable before action, normal cancellation applies without bonus replacement attack.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Does not interrupt already-resolving actions or remove Stunned. Resolve simultaneous priority with ordinary stable initiative/ties. No additional shared status.

### Visuals

Gust lifts active ally EXISTING action marker forward in visible turn order. No duplicated attack marker or extra head badge.

### Progression / notes

Once-per-Clash, no mastery numerical scaling selected. Replaces old6/5/4/3 cooldown and impossible multiple-active target condition. Owner selected13A. Reserve support priority hook must preserve one normal action/side and source once-use ledger; test Stunned and forced-replacement order.
