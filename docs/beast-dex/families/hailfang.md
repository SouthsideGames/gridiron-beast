## Football: Frost Nip

Ability ID: `ICE-02-F`. Category: Targeted effect.

Tracked route receiver reaches two-yard separation before pass release

Pre-pass separation triggers targeted current-play Slowed snowball

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Keep CB for both forms. While ready before pass release, tracked route-running receiver reaches at least TWO yards separation, no cut requirement (owner 2B).

### Targets / placement

Assigned receiver in man coverage; currently tracked receiver in zone. Must remain within FOUR yards at activation.

### Core resolution

Attempt Slowed: Frostpup 15%, Hoarhound 20% max movement-speed reduction. No direct damage, automatic PBU or separate evasion roll; normal shared-status gate.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5/4/3/2 subsequent defensive plays. Firing spends readiness even if existing status blocks application.

### Lifetime / persistence

B: Receiver must remain eligible until snowball impact. Pass release before impact cancels the snowball and Slowed application; play ending also cancels it. Firing still spends readiness. No added random miss chance. If applied, Slowed lasts current play only and clears at whistle.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Stunned or Knocked Down prevents source activation and preserves readiness. At impact recheck tracked route receiver, two-yard separation, four-yard range and pre-release eligibility. If no longer eligible, cancel without applying status; launched use remains spent.

### Visuals

Owner override: FIRE A SNOWBALL at receiver. Standard Slowed badge only if applied. No frozen legs, ground field or jaw-breath alternative.

### Progression / notes

Cold Trail route-reveal proposal withdrawn. Evolution keeps common rarity and CB. Different from Water-zone Stumbled and maximum-speed-neutral current-speed loss.

## Beast Clash: Cold Welcome

Ability ID: `ICE-02-C`. Category: Voluntary-entry disruption.

Successful enemy voluntary rotation while source active and ready

Incoming enemy after entry effects receives attempted Slowed

Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Source ACTIVE and not Stunned. Successful enemy voluntary rotation only; exclude forced rotation and KO replacement.

### Targets / placement

Incoming replacement AFTER all its entry effects resolve. Source must still be able to act; otherwise preserve readiness.

### Core resolution

Attempt Slowed 15% Frostpup / 20% Hoarhound initiative reduction. No damage, separate evasion roll or extra normal action.

### Recharge / frequency

Cooldown 5/4/3/2 subsequent source normal turns; reserve pauses, Stunned skips count. Throwing spends readiness even if status blocked.

### Lifetime / persistence

TWO recipient normal turns, application turn excluded. Recipient reserve pauses countdown.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Ordinary shared-status rules. Incoming Shielded or any different status blocks Slowed; do not strip protection. No rotation prevention or pursuit damage.

### Visuals

Snowball at incoming enemy then standard Slowed badge if applied.

### Progression / notes

Disruptor retained. Riptide Pursuit targets departing enemy with damage; this affects arrival initiative after entry protection.
