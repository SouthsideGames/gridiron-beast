Source: workbook v18. Apply the later shared overrides in [Approved Rules](../APPROVED_RULES.md) and the Task 8 specification.

### Trigger / eligibility

Lumini; common; WR; Clash archetype Support → Support. Ready own normal action replaces attack if at least two living allies are injured.

### Targets / placement

Two lowest HP-percentage living allies including reserves/self, stable tie order.

### Core resolution

Heal each 7 / 10% own max HP by Lumini / Photara. No surplus shield, cleanse or HP equalization.

If fewer than two injured, ordinary attack preserves. Cast spent even if Healing Reduced blocks all healing.

Immediate HP restoration; no recurring status or duration.

### Exceptions / counterplay

Normal maxHP cap/Healing Reduced; no revive. Cannot heal same ally twice when only one injured.

### Visuals

Two firefly lights split from source into selected allies; ordinary green numbers.

### Progression / notes

Cooldown 6 / 5 / 4 / 3 owner turns. Needs two distinct recipient selection before resolving heals; no retarget after first heal.
