## Findings to resolve

1. **Apex earning conflict.** 0/1,000 all-99 by season 15 in either controlled schedule. Even an optimistic resource upper bound cannot meet the goal within the authorized training-share range. XP tuning cannot fix a training shortfall already measured assuming immediate level 50. A new design ruling is required to change that constraint; do not silently change ordinary ceilings, natural growth, Temper, the calendar or Apex mechanics.
2. **Mastery pacing/exposure.** Twenty-season engine-generated counters show wide differences. Twenty-nine ordinary monsters reach football Mastered in the first observed season; passive/zone categories often take longer. Many common Clash individuals have no recorded use at all. Match actual sustained exposure before certifying the 3–6-season gate. No participation credit or active-execution cap was invented.
3. **Money and roster completion.** Average AI spare cap room rises from $353,407 at year 5 to $401,934 at year 20 across the three seeds. Two seed-2 post-offseason rosters have no kicker despite spare cap room; the weekly repair path exists, and Eastport subsequently plays normal year-9 scores. These observations warrant in-season pressure and start-of-season coverage review, not an automatic cap cut or an unproven claim of forfeits.
4. **Ordinary pancakes.** Final reconciled default results: 0.690 per base starting OL/game; 49.24% success; 11.60% of attempts lose the block. Frequency point estimate is above 0.6, with a 95% interval overlapping that boundary. A report-only qmax 0.12 candidate gives 0.550 / 48.84% / 9.73%, inside all three point targets. It is inside the approved parameter range, but uncertainty still warrants broader league-weighted confirmation and regression before integration. No production patch was applied.
5. **Performance acceptance.** No clean CPU pass can be claimed from a noisy host, and no rendering/mobile pass can be claimed without measured frames. Keep all existing effects and rules; investigate cost without changing outcomes.
