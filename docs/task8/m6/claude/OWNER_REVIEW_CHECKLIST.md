visual and wording review that only you can sign off.

## Ability descriptions

Each ability shows a name, a one-paragraph summary and a "Full rules" fold-out.

- **Football summaries** are the Ability Reviewer's "expected result" lines, written for players.
- **Clash summaries** are the Beast Dex mechanic line, written for designers. These are the ones most likely to need rewriting.
- **"Full rules"** is the Dex contract under seven plain headings (When it happens, Who or where, What it does, How often,
  How long it lasts, Limits and counterplay, Forms and mastery). It is accurate but dense.

Suggested sample, two from each element is enough to judge the pattern:

- [ ] Open six football abilities you know well. Is each summary correct and readable?
- [ ] Open six Clash abilities. Mark any summary a player could not follow.
- [ ] Open "Full rules" on three. Keep it, shorten it, or hide it from players?
- [ ] "See it" on three football abilities: the demonstration matches the summary.
- [ ] Decide: rewrite all 72 Clash summaries for players, rewrite only the ones you mark, or ship as they are.
