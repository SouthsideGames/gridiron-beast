# Per-ability field clocks and creation eligibility (B20)

**Scope.** All 19 football abilities that create a field, built from the approved contracts: Dex v18 with the N7b and N7c
overrides. Quotes are verbatim. Each row's clock and creation rule is the ability's own (N7c B20: "Preserve per-ability field clocks
and creation eligibility").

**Rulings applied to every row:**
- Kickoff catches are not receptions; kickoffs are not defensive snaps.
- A cooldown cannot cancel an already-earned queued field: the field waits under the contract's own pending rule and is not deleted.

**Status column:**
- **Clear:** the contract fixes the rule.
- **G1–G3:** resolved by owner ruling N7d (2026-11-07 10:21); see the end.

## Resolved gaps (owner ruling N7d, 2026-11-07 10:21)

- **G1:** Impact Pyre, Burning Wake and Tsunami Line count every completed scrimmage play by either team, including incompletions. Special-teams plays do not count. Explicit creation-play and halftime exceptions are preserved.
- **G2:** Runway and Beacon Lane decrement only when their creator participates in an offensive scrimmage play. Their field effects stay active while the countdown is paused.
- **G3:** the eight listed abilities wait for their creator's next eligible on-field snap. Frostmark, Echo Landing, Gear Grid, Tsunami Line and Splashdown keep their explicit off-field creation permissions.

## Original gap statements (historical)

### G1 — Which plays count for the lifetime?

**Abilities:** Impact Pyre, Burning Wake, Tsunami Line.

**Missing rule.** Their clocks say only "completed plays". The contract doesn't say whether these count:
- the other team's plays;
- kickoffs (now live);
- formula punts, field goals and extra points.

Splashdown says so explicitly ("regardless possession"); these three do not.

**Affected implementation:** WP5 field clocks for these three abilities; the B4 field limit.

**Recommendation:** every completed scrimmage play by either team counts; special-teams plays do not. Tsunami Line already
excludes special teams at creation.

### G2 — Whose participation?

**Abilities:** Runway, Beacon Lane.

**Missing rule.** Their lifetimes count "participating offensive scrimmage plays", but the contract doesn't say whose
participation: the creator's, or the field team's offense.

**Affected implementation:** WP5 field clocks for these two abilities.

**Recommendation:** the creator's participation, matching Runway's own cooldown wording ("subsequent participating offensive
plays"). The field pauses while its creator is off the field.

### G3 — Can the field spawn with its creator off the field?

**Abilities:** Burning Wake, Bloom Break, Bramble Trap, Beacon Lane, Bulwark Grid, Shadow Patch, Crosswind Rescue, Mind Bend.

**Missing rule.** These contracts don't say whether the field spawns when its creator is not on the field at the spawn snap.
For example, TEs are absent in Spread and a second S is present only in Nickel.

**Affected implementation:** WP6 creation hooks for these eight abilities.

**Recommendation:**
- The field waits as pending until the creator's next participating snap.
- That matches the "next participating" wording of Charge Strip, Static Field, Permafrost and Runway, and Burning Wake's need for Pyroclast's initial position.
- Only the contracts that explicitly allow bench creation spawn without the creator: Frostmark, Echo Landing, Gear Grid, Tsunami Line and Splashdown.
