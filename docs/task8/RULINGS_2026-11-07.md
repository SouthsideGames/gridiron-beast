## Interpretations recorded for implementation (flag any that are wrong)

- **P2 "over 7 yards" / "over 4 yards" / "any positive rushing gain":** a completed official rushing attempt qualifies at ≥ 8, ≥ 5 and ≥ 1 whole yards of net gain for Flaroo, Blazynx and Infernox respectively. The threshold follows the form at the time of the carry; mastery does not change it.
- **P8 "A Stunned skip consumes that first-turn penalty":** if the entrant's first normal turn is skipped by Stunned, the half-strength penalty is spent on that skipped turn and its next performed turn is at full strength.
- **P8 "nonnumeric effects remain usable":** effects without a magnitude (for example sweeps, setups or riders) apply normally on the penalised turn; only damage multipliers, heals, shields and status-application chances are halved.
- **P10:** the dodge and the afterimage are rolled once per whole enemy normal attack (all hits of a multi-hit attack together), so "every hit must miss" is satisfied by one successful roll; explicitly undodgeable attacks bypass them without a roll.
